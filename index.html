Meet Mohand & The Ihmadi Legacy

Welcome to Ihmadi (Alnif Earth), the undisputed epicenter of global palaeontology nestled in the arid Anti-Atlas mountain range of southern Morocco. Founded and led by Mohand, a lifelong native geologist and field specialist, our center is dedicated to preserving, researching, and sharing the legendary deep-time treasures of Morocco.

For decades, Mohand has guided international paleontologists, academic researchers, film crews, and curious travelers across the famous Paleozoic and Mesozoic fossil beds of Alnif and the Kem Kem basin. With his unparalleled local knowledge, Mohand transforms every expedition from a simple field tour into an immersive educational journey through deep time, Berber culture, and traditional Saharan hospitality.

Earth History & Stratigraphy

Morocco’s Geological Eras

Southern Morocco is widely recognized by geologists worldwide as an open-air paleontology museum. Explore the four fundamental geological epochs that shaped Alnif and the surrounding regions.

541 – 485 Ma

Cambrian Era

The Cambrian Explosion

Witness the origin of complex ocean life. Moroccan Cambrian strata reveal massive Paradoxides trilobites, primitive echinoderms, and calcified marine mudstones formed along ancient Gondwanan sea margins.

  • • Key Fossils: Paradoxides, Cambropallas
  • • Site Locations: Jbel Wawrmast & Alnif Basin
  • • Rock Type: Green shale & limestone beds
View Excursions
485 – 443 Ma

Ordovician Era

Great Marine Diversification

Famous for soft-bodied preservation in the Fezouata Formation near Zagora and Alnif. Features Flexicalymene, Asaphida trilobites, giant sea scorpions (Eurypterids), and early bryozoans.

  • • Key Fossils: Flexicalymene, Colpocoryphe
  • • Site Locations: Zagora & Jbel Tiskaouine
  • • Rock Type: Fine siltstone & quartzites
View Excursions
Alnif Specialty
419 – 359 Ma

Devonian Era

Golden Age of Trilobites

Alnif is world-renowned for Devonian marine reefs. Discover breathtaking spiny trilobites including Dicranurus monster, Walliserops trident, Phacopida, and Scutelluidae preserved in dense limestone matrices.

  • • Key Fossils: Dicranurus, Walliserops, Phacops
  • • Site Locations: Hamar Laghdad & Couvinian Beds
  • • Rock Type: Hard pink & dark limestone
View Excursions
145 – 66 Ma

Cretaceous Era

The Kem Kem River Beds

Travel to the famous Kem Kem beds south of Alnif. Unearth dinosaur teeth and bones from Spinosaurus aegyptiacus, Carcharodontosaurus, sawfish Onchopristis, and giant Cretaceous crocodilians.

  • • Key Fossils: Spinosaurus teeth, Enchodus fish
  • • Site Locations: Kem Kem Escarpment & Taouz
  • • Rock Type: Red sandstone & deltaic silt

Fieldwork & Excavation

Deep-Time Field Excursions

Duration: 1 to 5 Days 4x4 Off-Road Transport Included Beginner to Academic Expert

Join Mohand on direct field trips into the rugged mountains surrounding Alnif. Drive in rugged 4x4 vehicles to remote Devonian limestone beds and Cretaceous sandstone ridges. Hand-crack rock strata using professional rock hammers, locate embedded trilobite fossils, learn stratification reading, and take your findings to the lab for precision cleaning.

Equipment Provided: Rock hammers, chisels, protective eyewear, field notebooks, and specimen bags.
Recommended Packing: Sturdy hiking boots, wide-brim hat, sun lotion, field gloves, and lightweight layer clothing.
Starting from €120 / day / group

Physical Center & Lab

Ihmadi Trilobites Center (Alnif Earth Museum)

Located directly in Alnif, our physical museum showcases hundreds of world-class fossil specimens carefully excavated from the surrounding Paleozoic strata. From delicate, needle-spined Devonian Dicranurus to massive 80cm Paradoxides trilobites and predatory Spinosaurus teeth.

Interactive Laboratory Viewing

Step into our active cleaning workshop and watch master preparators use pneumatic air scribes and micro-abrasive sandblasters under magnification stereomicroscopes to free delicate fossils from solid rock matrix without damage.

Authentic Specimen Handling

Unlike conventional glass-case museums, visitors at Ihmadi are invited to hold genuine 400-million-year-old trilobite carapaces and dinosaur fossils, feeling the exact texture of ancient marine armor and tooth enamel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers regarding tour inclusions, gear, field safety, weather conditions, custom itineraries, and legal fossil transport.

Our guided field trips include 4x4 off-road transport to fossil beds, an expert guide (Mohand), geological tools (rock hammers, chisels, eye protection), field permits where required, packed drinking water, Berber home-cooked lunch, and laboratory preparation viewing at the Ihmadi Center.
The optimal seasons for field exploration in southern Morocco are Autumn (October to December) and Spring (February to May), when desert temperatures are mild and pleasant (20°C–28°C). Summer months (July–August) can be extremely hot, but early morning field sessions and afternoon laboratory sessions can easily be arranged.
